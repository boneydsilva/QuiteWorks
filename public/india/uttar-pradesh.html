The practical bits, for Uttar Pradesh

  • The power and the broadband. The server PC needs to be switched on during working hours; a small UPS is enough. The internet can be down all day and nobody in the office would notice.
  • Buying it. ₹14,999 once, by UPI, bank transfer or card, with a GST invoice made out to your business. There is no renewal you have to pay.
  • Getting it running. Installed remotely over a call, from anywhere in Uttar Pradesh — about ten minutes for the server and under a minute per employee PC. Guided setup is available if you would rather not touch it.
  • Support. Email, answered within one working day, IST, by the person who wrote the software. Ask in हिन्दी or in English.
