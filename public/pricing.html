Questions

About buying

Why is it this cheap?

Because we are not paying for your servers, your storage or your backups — you already own the PC it runs on. Nearly all of what a cloud task tool charges you every month is infrastructure and the sales team needed to keep filling it. WorkQueue has neither.

What if we grow from 8 people to 40?

The price does not change — install it on the new machines and carry on. Do check the requirements though: WorkQueue is built and tested for a single office of roughly 25 machines at once. Past that, talk to us first.

Can we pay monthly instead?

No, and that is deliberate. A monthly plan would mean building the billing, the licence checks and the enforcement that the price is low precisely because we skipped. If the one-off amount is difficult, ask — we would rather split it in two than lose you.

Do you do custom work?

Yes. If WorkQueue is nearly right but your business needs one specific thing it does not do, describe it — sometimes it becomes a feature for everybody, sometimes it is a paid piece of work. Either way you will get a straight answer and a fixed quote.

What happens to our data if we stop paying?

Nothing, because there is nothing to stop paying and your data was never anywhere but your own PC. This is the practical difference between owning software and renting it.
