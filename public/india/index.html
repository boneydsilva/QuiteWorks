Across India

Built in Mumbai. Meant for every office in the country.

WorkQueue does not care which city you are in, because it never leaves your office — it runs on the PCs you already own, over your own Wi-Fi. What does change from place to place is the language your team works in, so the site is written in English and eleven Indian languages, and there is a page for every state and union territory.

Pick your state

Each page is about that state — its working towns, the kinds of business that are common there, and the language most of its offices run on.

Why this suits an Indian small business in particular

It survives your broadband

Outages, throttling, a fibre cut down the road — none of it stops work, because nothing needed the internet in the first place. Everything runs over the office LAN or Wi-Fi.

One price in rupees, with a GST invoice

₹14,999 once for the whole company, paid by UPI, bank transfer or card, with a proper GST invoice for your books. No dollar subscription that moves with the exchange rate.

Your team is not asked to learn an app

Staff who will never open a project-management tool will still read a strip that is already on their screen. That is the whole reason it is 34 pixels at the top rather than another icon on the desktop.

It reads in the language of the floor

The site explains itself in eleven Indian languages so the person deciding, the person paying and the person actually using it can each read it in the language they think in.
